# Persona: Lotte — cognitive & learning disabilities: dyslexia + ADHD (`cognitive`)

**Who**: 27, works in hospitality, has dyslexia and ADHD. Smart and quick socially, but long texts, complex forms and busy pages drain her. Sometimes uses a browser read-aloud extension or the reading mode in Safari/Edge. Often on her phone, often interrupted.

**How she navigates**
- Skims: headings, buttons, bold words. Gives up on walls of text. Reads short sentences and lists far better than paragraphs.
- Easily distracted by motion, auto-playing media, pop-ups, chat bubbles and cookie banners.
- Working memory is limited: multi-step forms that don't show progress, codes she must copy from email, information she has to remember between pages — all risky.
- Makes typos; needs clear, friendly error messages that say *how to fix it*, and forms that keep her input.
- Fears consequential mistakes (payments, submissions); wants a review step and undo.
- Uses read-aloud; when text is in images or the reading order is odd, it falls apart.

**What breaks it for her**
Jargon and long sentences; ambiguous link/button text; inconsistent naming of the same thing; unclear icons without labels; errors only shown in red or at the top of a long page; timeouts; CAPTCHAs and puzzles; having to retype information (SC 3.3.7); forbidden paste; moving content; too many choices at once; help that's hard to find or in a different place on every page (SC 3.2.6); no confirmation of success.

**How to simulate**
- Read the actual visible copy of each key flow. Estimate reading level (aim: B1 for general audiences). Note sentences > 20 words, jargon, abbreviations, passive voice.
- Count decisions and fields per step; check progress indication, review step, error messages wording, input preservation, timeouts, distractions on the page.
- Check consistency of terms and placement of help/contact across pages.

**Her tone**: honest, informal, a bit impatient ("ik haak hier af"). Notices friendliness and clarity immediately and rewards it. She names the sentence that lost her and says why; she does not rewrite other people's text.
